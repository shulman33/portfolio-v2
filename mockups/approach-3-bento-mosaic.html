Bento Mosaic

Screenshot-forward and dense. Projects become full-bleed image tiles in an asymmetric bento grid — one large feature tile anchors it, the rest vary in size. Each tile carries a gradient veil with the title always visible; the description reveals on hover as the image saturates, zooms, and gains a green ring. Most visual of the three; the screenshots do the talking.
