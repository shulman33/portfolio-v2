Editorial Showcase

A more immersive, magazine-style layout. Each project gets a full-width row with a large screenshot framed in a perspective-tilted browser window that straightens and lifts with a green glow on hover. Rows alternate left/right. Ghosted outline numerals and oversized Syne headlines give it scale and editorial weight — best when the visuals deserve to be big.
